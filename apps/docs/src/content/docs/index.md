ccpilot is a digital prosthetic for the executive center, transforming academic noise into the Single Logical Next Step.

## What is CCPILOT

Traditional planning is a vertical climb. You start at the bottom with a mountain of requirements and must climb through gathering and integration just to see your first task. 

**CCPILOT is horizontal.** We clear the field of noise, integrate the data in the background, and place you directly at the start of the path. You don't climb to see the view; you walk the path and look back when you're ready.
